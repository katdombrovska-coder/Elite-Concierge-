How It Works

Live in Days,
Not Months.

01

Discovery & blueprint

We map your current call flows, FAQs, and booking systems in a single 30-minute session. You leave with a one-page agent blueprint — every intent, every fallback, every handoff.

02

Our team writes your agent's voice, plugs into your CRM and calendar, and connects your existing phone number. You review a working agent within 3–5 business days.
