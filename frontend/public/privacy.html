Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to users of Elite AI Space services and platforms.

1. Who We Are

Elite AI Space is an AI automation and communication infrastructure company operated by Elite AI, based in Portugal.

We build AI-powered voice, chat, and workflow automation systems for businesses, including AI employees, AI receptionists, and communication automation infrastructures.

For privacy-related inquiries contact: hello@eliteai.space

2. What Data We Collect

We may collect personal and business information provided directly by users, including:

  • name
  • email address
  • phone number
  • company information
  • booking and contact form submissions
  • onboarding information
  • communication preferences

We may also collect operational and technical data generated through service usage, including:

  • AI call recordings and transcripts
  • chat conversation logs
  • appointment and workflow metadata
  • timestamps and interaction history
  • analytics and website usage information
  • device and browser information

3. Why We Process Data

We process personal data for purposes including:

  • delivering AI communication services
  • onboarding and supporting clients
  • operating AI voice and chat systems
  • improving service performance and reliability
  • customer support and communication
  • security, fraud prevention, and legal compliance

Legal bases under GDPR may include:

  • performance of a contract
  • legitimate interests
  • legal obligations
  • user consent where required

We do not use personal data for automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects without human oversight.

4. AI Call & Chat Data

Elite AI Space may process voice calls, chat conversations, and AI-generated interaction data on behalf of business clients.

These interactions may be recorded and transcribed for:

  • quality assurance
  • service optimization
  • workflow automation
  • compliance and security
  • AI performance improvement

Where required by law, users may be informed that conversations are recorded or processed by AI systems.

Data is accessible only to authorized personnel and relevant business clients.

5. Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations.

Typical retention periods include:

  • enquiry and contact form data: up to 24 months
  • client account information: contract duration plus legal retention periods
  • call recordings and transcripts: typically up to 12 months unless otherwise agreed
  • analytics data: anonymized or aggregated where possible
